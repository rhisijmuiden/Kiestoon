# Features

Everything Kiestoon can do, with a screenshot of each part. The screenshots come from a real Kiestoon with a fictional office and are made again with every change. Click a heading for the full chapter.

## Telephony

### [Live dashboard](index.md)

Calls in progress, registered phones, trunks and queues at a glance.

![Live dashboard](shot:dashboard)

### [Extensions](extensions.md)

One page per extension: phones, voicemail, forwarding, recording and the linked user.

![Extensions](shot:extensions)

### [Ring groups](ring-groups.md)

Ring several phones at once or one after the other, with a fallback when nobody answers.

![Ring groups](shot:ring-groups)

### [Call queues](queues.md)

Agents, strategies, hold music, position announcements and a callback instead of waiting.

![Call queues](shot:queues)

### [Visual IVR builder](ivr.md)

Draw the call flow on a canvas and connect the blocks by dragging lines.

![Visual IVR builder](shot:ivr-canvas)

### [Spoken menus and speech input](ivr.md)

Type the text of a menu and let it be spoken; callers can also say what they want.

![Spoken menus and speech input](shot:ivr-speech)

### [Time conditions and holidays](time-conditions.md)

Office hours per route, with Dutch public holidays filled in for you.

![Time conditions and holidays](shot:time-conditions)

### [Conference rooms](conferences.md)

Dial-in rooms with a PIN, a moderator and an optional recording.

![Conference rooms](shot:conferences)

### [On-call](on-call.md)

A weekly or daily rota that rings the engineer on duty on their own mobile, escalates to the backup and the team
lead, and lets engineers swap shifts in the portal.

![On-call](shot:on-call)

### [Voice prompts and ElevenLabs voices](prompts.md)

Record, upload or generate prompts with a local voice or an ElevenLabs voice.

![Voice prompts and ElevenLabs voices](shot:voice-picker)

### [Voicemail and fax to e-mail](fax.md)

Voicemail with transcription and incoming faxes as PDF in your mailbox.

![Voicemail and fax to e-mail](shot:fax)

### [Call history and call journey](call-journey.md)

Search every call and see its route step by step: menu, queue, who answered.

![Call history and call journey](shot:call-journey)

### [Recordings with AI summaries](ai.md)

Listen back, read the transcript and a short summary, locally or with Claude.

![Recordings with AI summaries](shot:recording-ai)

### [Call quality](call-quality.md)

MOS, jitter and packet loss per call, with alerts when quality drops.

![Call quality](shot:call-quality)

### [Feature codes](feature-codes.md)

Codes for parking, pickup, do not disturb, hot desks and more, all adjustable.

![Feature codes](shot:features)

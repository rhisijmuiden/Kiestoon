    Features¶

    Everything Kiestoon can do, with a screenshot of each part. The screenshots come from a real Kiestoon with a fictional office and are made again with every change. Click a heading for the full chapter.

    Telephony¶

    Live dashboard¶

    Calls in progress, registered phones, trunks and queues at a glance.

    Extensions¶

    One page per extension: phones, voicemail, forwarding, recording and the linked user.

    Ring groups¶

    Ring several phones at once or one after the other, with a fallback when nobody answers.

    Call queues¶

    Agents, strategies, hold music, position announcements and a callback instead of waiting.

    Visual IVR builder¶

    Draw the call flow on a canvas and connect the blocks by dragging lines.

    Spoken menus and speech input¶

    Type the text of a menu and let it be spoken; callers can also say what they want.

    Time conditions and holidays¶

    Office hours per route, with Dutch public holidays filled in for you.

    Conference rooms¶

    Dial-in rooms with a PIN, a moderator and an optional recording.

    On-call¶

    A weekly or daily rota that rings the engineer on duty on their own mobile, escalates to the backup and the team lead, and lets engineers swap shifts in the portal.

    Voice prompts and ElevenLabs voices¶

    Record, upload or generate prompts with a local voice or an ElevenLabs voice.

    Voicemail and fax to e-mail¶

    Voicemail with transcription and incoming faxes as PDF in your mailbox.

    Call history and call journey¶

    Search every call and see its route step by step: menu, queue, who answered.

    Recordings with AI summaries¶

    Listen back, read the transcript and a short summary, locally or with Claude.

    Call quality¶

    MOS, jitter and packet loss per call, with alerts when quality drops.

    Feature codes¶

    Codes for parking, pickup, do not disturb, hot desks and more, all adjustable.

    Providers and Teams¶

    Trunks with provider templates¶

    Ready-made templates for Voys, Cheapconnect, Speakup, KPN and Vodafone Ziggo, with a guide for each.

    Microsoft Teams Direct Routing¶

    Teams users call and are called through Kiestoon, as an extension like any other.

    Phones¶

    Automatic provisioning¶

    Yealink, Snom, Fanvil, Grandstream, Gigaset, Poly, Htek, Akuvox and Cisco: plug in, link, done.

    Phone keys and expansion modules¶

    A visual key editor with modules, key templates and intercom.

    Reception¶

    Switchboard¶

    Every call and colleague on one screen; transfer by dragging.

    Wallboard and TV screens¶

    Queue figures and agents live on a TV, with a designer and its own address per screen.

    Alerts and scheduled reports¶

    Alerts when the service level drops, and reports by e-mail.

    Phone book¶

    Shared contacts on every phone, in the softphone and for caller names.

    For every employee¶

    User portal¶

    Do not disturb, forwarding, follow me, own hours and voicemail, all in one place.

    Colleagues and drag-and-drop transfer¶

    See who is available and transfer a call by dragging it onto a colleague.

    Windows softphone¶

    Tray app with contacts, presence, queues and headset buttons.

    Mobile app¶

    Calls on your phone with push notifications, linked with a QR code.

    Multi-site and high availability¶

    Branch offices with Kiestoon Edge¶

    A Raspberry Pi connects a branch office through WireGuard, without port forwarding.

    Linked systems and follow the sun¶

    One phone book across countries, and calls follow the office that is open.

    High availability¶

    An active/passive pair that takes over within seconds.

    Security¶

    Users, roles, MFA and single sign-on¶

    Mandatory MFA for administrators, passkeys, and sign-in with Entra ID, Google or OIDC/SAML.

    Firewall and fraud protection¶

    nftables firewall with safe mode, fail2ban, geo-blocking and trusted networks.

    Internet access and SSL¶

    Let's Encrypt with the built-in Caddy, or your own reverse proxy.

    Audit log¶

    Who changed what and when.

    System and integrations¶

    CRM integrations and call pop-up¶

    See the customer from your CRM as the phone rings, and log calls automatically.

    API, webhooks and metrics¶

    REST API with tokens, signed webhooks and Prometheus metrics.

    Backups and restore¶

    Full and incremental backups to FTP, FTPS, SFTP, SCP and more, and restore from there.

    Own mail server¶

    Your own SMTP server with TLS and OAuth2 for all mail Kiestoon sends.

    Updates from the portal¶

    Version info, check for updates and update, on a production or testing channel.

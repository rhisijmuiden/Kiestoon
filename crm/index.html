    CRM integrations and call pop-up¶

    Kiestoon links to HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive and Exact Online. While the phone rings, a pop-up shows who is calling according to the CRM, and every call is written back to the customer in the CRM automatically. Nothing changes for installations without a CRM.

    What it does¶

    • Recognising callers. As soon as a call from outside comes in, Kiestoon looks the number up in the CRMs in the order you set. Numbers are compared on their last 9 digits, so 06…, +316… and 00316… all match the same contact. Results are kept for 10 minutes.
    • Pop-up. In the web portal (on every page), the Windows softphone and the switchboard. It shows the contact, the company, open deals and tickets, the last contact and the owner, with a button to open the record in the CRM. When the browser phone rings, it also has Answer and Decline. An unknown number gets a button to create the contact.
    • Name on desk phones. The caller name on the phone display becomes Jan de Vries - Bakkerij De Vries. The call waits at most 1.5 seconds for the CRM; when it is slower the phone rings with the number only.
    • Logging calls. Every call with a customer becomes an activity on the contact (and company), under the colleague who took or made it: direction, duration, outcome and the number called. Missed calls can become a Call back task.
    • After the call the pop-up asks for a note, the outcome and an optional follow-up task. The note is added to the activity that was logged automatically.
    • Recordings never leave the server. The CRM gets a link that needs a Kiestoon login. With AI summaries of calls switched on (AI), the summary is added to the activity a few minutes later.

    Only the caller's phone number goes to the CRM to look it up. Tokens and secrets are stored on the server and never shown again.

    Connecting a CRM¶

    Open System › Integrations › CRM and click Connect on the CRM.

    HubSpot¶

    1. In HubSpot: Settings › Integrations › Private apps › Create a private app.
    2. Scopes: crm.objects.contacts.read and .write, crm.objects.companies.read, crm.objects.deals.read, tickets, crm.objects.owners.read.
    3. Paste the access token in Kiestoon and save. Kiestoon checks the token at once.

    Salesforce¶

    1. In Setup: External Client App Manager › New. Enable OAuth with the scope Manage user data via APIs (api).
    2. Turn on the client credentials flow and pick a run as user that may read contacts and accounts and create tasks.
    3. Fill in your My Domain URL (https://acme.my.salesforce.com), the consumer key and the consumer secret.

    Pipedrive¶

    1. In Pipedrive: your name › Personal preferences › API, copy the token. Use an account that sees all persons.
    2. Fill in the company domain (the part before .pipedrive.com) and the token.

    Pipedrive organisations have no phone field, so Kiestoon looks up persons only.

    Exact Online¶

    1. In the Exact App Center: Manage apps › Register a test or production app. Use the redirect URI that Kiestoon shows in the form (https://<your PBX>/api/crm/exact/callback).
    2. Fill in the country, the client ID and the client secret, then click Sign in with Exact and sign in with an Exact user that may see relations.
    3. Kiestoon refreshes the sign-in itself. Exact has no phone search, so Kiestoon keeps a list of the phone numbers of all contacts and accounts and refreshes it every hour.

    Exact has no call activity: calls are logged as a communication note on the relation.

    The PBX address

    The redirect URI and the links to recordings use the PBX address from Separate host names or Internet access. Set it before you register the Exact app.

    Settings per CRM¶

    Setting What
    Look up contacts / companies Which records are searched for the caller's number
    Show in the pop-up Open deals, open tickets, last activity, owner, lifecycle stage
    Log every call automatically Inbound and outbound, only inbound, or only answered calls
    Missed calls become a task A Call back task for the owner of the contact
    Add a link to the recording When the call was recorded
    Add the AI summary When AI summaries of calls are on
    Create a contact for unknown numbers Off by default: colleagues create one from the pop-up
    Users ↔ owners Matched on e-mail address; pick an owner by hand where it differs

    For all CRMs¶

    • Look-up order: the first connected CRM that knows the number wins. Unknown numbers fall back to the phone book.
    • Show the CRM name on desk phones (on by default).
    • Pop-up for everyone whose phone rings: also for colleagues in a queue or ring group. Off: only the colleague who answers sees it.

    Sync log and call history¶

    The CRM sync log lists the look-ups and writes of the last 30 days. When a CRM cannot be reached, Kiestoon tries again after 1, 5 and 30 minutes and then every hour, for 24 hours; then the admins get a mail and you can Retry now. When a CRM refuses the stored sign-in, its card says Sign in again and look-ups skip it.

    The call history gets a CRM column: Logged, Task created, Retrying or Not a customer. Click it to open the record in the CRM.

    To test a number without calling, use Test a number on the CRM tab.

    Other software¶

    For a CRM that is not in the list, use the API and webhooks: the call.ringing webhook for a pop-up in your own software and POST /api/calls for click-to-call.

    Hot desks¶

    A hot desk is a desk phone that anyone can use as their own: they log in with their extension number and PIN, and the phone loads their account. Calls to their extension ring there, and calls they make show their number.

    Setting up a hot desk¶

    1. Create an extension for the desk, for example 290 Hot desk 1. This is the guest account the phone uses while nobody is logged in. You can give it limited outgoing rights.
    2. Go to Phones & provisioning, click Edit on the phone and choose that extension.
    3. Under Hot desk, switch on This phone is a hot desk. Switch on Log out every night if desks should be free every morning (everyone is logged out at 03:00).

    Hot desking works on every provisioned desk phone. It has been built for Yealink.

    Logging in and out¶

    Code What it does
    *55 Log in: enter your extension number and #, then your PIN and #
    *56 Log out: the phone goes back to its guest account

    The PIN is the voicemail PIN of the extension. An extension without a PIN cannot log in.

    After you log in or out, the phone reloads its settings within a few seconds. You can put *55 and *56 on a speed dial key to make it one press.

    • An extension is logged in on one hot desk at a time: logging in on another desk logs it out on the first one.
    • The keys of the phone stay the same; the account on line 1 changes.
    • Your own desk phone and the softphone keep working. If the extension allows only one device at a time (Devices at the same time 1), the hot desk takes over from the other device.
    • After 5 wrong PINs, the desk is locked for 10 minutes.

    In the admin console¶

    The phone list shows who is logged in on each hot desk. Click the log out icon to free a desk. Every login and logout is in the audit log.
